$About
{topic|Elona Keys}
Classic Elona keybinds for Elin. Play with the keyboard instead of dragging the mouse around.

Item keys open a scrollable menu of matching items. Click an entry or press its letter to act. Directional actions ask "which direction?"; answer with a movement key, the wait key for yourself, or Escape to cancel.

{pair|Config|BepInEx/config/elin.elonakeys.cfg}
Every key is rebindable. Format: e, shift+t, period, comma-separated alternatives, or none to unbind.

By default these keys take priority over Elin's own bindings when they clash (OverrideGameKeys=true in the config). Set it to false to let the game's keys win instead.

$Keys
{topic|Item Keys}
{pair|g / numpad 0|Get everything on your tile}
{pair|d|Drop menu (stays open)}
{pair|e|Eat menu}
{pair|q|Quaff menu, includes nearby wells and fountains}
{pair|r|Read menu}
{pair|Z|Zap a rod, then a direction}
{pair|w|Wear/wield menu, click equipped items to remove}
{pair|t|Use an item}
{pair|T|Throw an item}
{pair|shift+x|Inventory}

{topic|Action Keys}
{pair|v|Cast (spell/ability list)}
{pair|i|Interact with a direction (talk, give, trade...)}
{pair|f|Fire ranged weapon}
{pair|b|Bash a direction}
{pair|D|Dig/mine a wall}
{pair|o|Open a container or door (lockpicks if locked)}
{pair|C|Close a door}
{pair|p|Pray}
{pair|O|Offer at an adjacent altar}
{pair|G|Give items to an adjacent character}

{topic|Not included}
・ Dip (B) and ammo switch (A) have no clean Elin equivalent yet.
・ Character sheet, journal, log and help already have native Elin keys.
・ Search (s) is not bound; Elin's own Search is a map text-search widget.
