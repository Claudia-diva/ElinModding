$About
{topic|Elona Keys}
Classic Elona keybinds for Elin. Play with the keyboard; the mouse becomes optional.

・ Item keys open a paginated menu: cursor with up/down (HOLD to keep scrolling), Enter or letter or click to act, left/right arrows switch pages.
・ The selected item's full stat card is shown automatically; press x on a row to open the examine window.
・ z inside a menu cycles sorting (original / name / weight or category / count or stock); the weight column sits on the right.
・ Shift returns to the previous window in a chain (wheel, give, equipment slots...), or closes the last one. Escape closes the WHOLE window stack at once (conversations and yes/no prompts keep their own Escape).
・ Stacks open a count spinner "n (max)": left/right +-1, up/down +-10, Enter confirms. numpad0 = act with the WHOLE stack, spinner skipped (drop all, take all; undroppable and important-marked items stay safe). On plain rows numpad0 holds the item in your hands instead (q, hover a potion, 0 = armed for throwing). Important-marked items show a gold star.
・ Hover or select an item/spell and press 0-9 to assign it to a hotkey (shift+digit for slots 11-20).
・ Directional actions highlight the nearby tiles and wait for a movement key (wait key = yourself, Escape/click = cancel).
・ Native game popups (right-click menus, yes/no dialogs, list windows) also take arrows + Enter.
・ Native windows: up/down move, left/right switch group (list/grid/dropdowns/buttons), z/x switch window tabs (character sheet, quest board, crafting), Enter acts, x examines in item grids. Tab stays native (chat) except inside window families.
・ Quest boards: up/down scroll the requests, left/right switch tabs, r = reroll.
・ Crafting (stations + quick craft): the normal window, keyboard-driven: up/down pick recipes (preview follows when the cursor rests), +/- count (shift = 10s), m = material picker (again = next slot), Enter crafts, Esc cancels a running craft, z/x categories. (CraftingStyle=menu for a list-style menu instead.)
・ Menu titles show the page, item count and total weight; the bottom line carries page/items/weight/slots. In-menu keys: up/down move (hold = scroll), left/right pages, Enter/letter/Space use, z sort (or z/x tabs in crafting/browser, hold x = reset), x examine, 1-9 or Ctrl+1-9 bind, Tab window family, Shift back.

{topic|Deliberate differences from Elona}
・ Quantity for stacks is an arrow-key spinner instead of typed numbers.
・ No Rest key: Elin rests via beds and the native Meditate key.
・ No dedicated look key: * targeting shows the same info card as mouse hover instead.
・ Journal (j), log and help stay on Elin's own keys.

{topic|Boundaries (mouse land, for now)}
・ Build/designation modes (construction, zone designations, deconstruct, move-installed, house editor) are mouse-driven; mod keys sleep there.
・ Pixel paint, casino games, the PCC editor and the jukebox are inherently mouse activities.
